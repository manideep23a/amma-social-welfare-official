ABOUT US

Compassion in action

AMMA Social Welfare Organisation works to support people in need and strengthen communities through practical social welfare activities.

Our focus is on humanitarian support, community assistance and initiatives that bring people together to help one another.

To serve with compassion, respond to genuine needs and encourage community participation in social welfare.

FOUNDER

Leadership with a heart for service

Driven by a commitment to social service and community welfare, the founder has worked to support people and encourage meaningful humanitarian initiatives.
